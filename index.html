RAY · Resilience Assistant for You

ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026 · OASIS Track A

A harness for GeoAI agents in disaster intelligence. Ask about any place, and RAY answers with maps, numbers and the sources behind them, for residents, planners and analysts.

面向灾害情报的 GeoAI 智能体框架。问任何一个地方，RAY 都会用地图、数字和背后的数据来源回答，服务居民、规划者和分析师。

  • Each analysis step is authorized by the data it reads每一步分析都由它读取的数据授权
  • Every number is checked against the result it cites每个数字都与它引用的结果逐一核对
  • Any run replays without the model任何一次运行都能脱离模型重放

Treasure Island, Florida, seen from orbit in November 2023 and again on 10 October 2024, the day after Hurricane Milton made landfall. Our models combine pairs like this with the view from the street to estimate damage, debris and recovery building by building. 佛罗里达州 Treasure Island，2023 年 11 月与 2024 年 10 月 10 日（飓风米尔顿登陆次日）的卫星影像。我们的模型把这样的影像对和街景结合起来，逐栋估算损毁、废墟量与恢复情况。
